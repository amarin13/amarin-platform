## The problem

Most teams believe their productivity is about how much effort they put in.

They track hours.  
They measure tasks completed.

But effort is not value.

The real challenge is identifying **leverage** — the impact your work has.

Without leverage, effort is wasted.

---

## What is leverage?

Leverage is the multiplier effect of your work.

A small change that unlocks big results.  
A feature that transforms user behavior.  
An automation that saves hundreds of hours.

High leverage means **high impact relative to effort**.

Teams often overlook leverage because it’s less visible than effort.

---

## What is effort?

Effort is the resources you spend: time, people, money.

It’s tangible. You can see it, measure it.

Effort feels productive.

But it can be misleading.

High effort doesn’t guarantee high impact.  
Low effort can yield outsized results.

---

## The leverage vs effort matrix

|                   | **Low Effort**          | **High Effort**          |
| ----------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------ |
| **High Leverage** | Quick wins, big impact  | Strategic bets, worth it |
| **Low Leverage**  | Busy work, distractions | Time sinks, waste        |

Most teams get stuck in **low leverage, high effort** projects.

They build complex solutions no one needs.

And they miss the **high leverage, low effort** opportunities.

---

## Why teams build the wrong things

Several traps lead teams astray:

- Mistaking busyness for progress
- Over-engineering features
- Lack of clear priorities
- Fear of risk and failure
- Ignoring data and feedback

Organizations reward visible effort, not outcomes.

This creates incentives to “do more” instead of “do right.”

---

## How to focus on leverage

Start by asking:

- What impact does this work create?
- How does effort compare to value?
- What data supports this priority?

Use frameworks like:

- Impact mapping
- Cost-benefit analysis
- Experimentation and validation

Leadership must clarify goals and encourage focusing on results.

---

## Examples

A SaaS team improved retention by 15% with a simple onboarding tweak — high leverage, low effort.

Another built a complex dashboard nobody used — low leverage, high effort.

After pivoting to automate manual imports, they saved hundreds of hours — high leverage, high effort.

---

## The takeaway

Effort alone is a poor measure of success.

Leverage drives impact.

Teams that focus on leverage build the right things.

They move faster.  
They deliver value.

Stop equating effort with progress.

Start prioritizing leverage.

---

## Closing

Build less.  
Build smart.  
Build leverage.

Because what you build matters more than how hard you build it.
